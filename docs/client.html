The Problem We're Solving

Problem Statement

Mountain meadows in the Cascade-Siskiyou region are critical for water storage, but climate change and historical degradation have caused many to dry out. Natural resource managers currently lack tools to systematically identify lost meadows or assess their restoration potential under future climate scenarios, limiting effective water resilience planning.

Our Solution

We trained machine learning algorithms on Oregon wetland databases to recognize the topographic, hydrologic, and climate signatures of mountain meadows. The tool analyzes these patterns across satellite imagery and terrain data to predict where lost meadows exist, even when they're now covered by forest. Interactive sliders let you adjust snowpack and drought levels to see how climate change affects restoration potential, while watershed filtering helps you focus on your management area.
